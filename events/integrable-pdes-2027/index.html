Integrable PDEs and Related Topics

In Honour of Prof. Patrick Gérard

Home Event Information Schedule of the Event

Overview

The past five years have seen rapid progress in integrable systems, opening up exciting directions and deepening their connections with other areas of partial differential equations. At the heart of this meeting is a week-long mini-course delivered by Professor Patrick Gérard of Université Paris-Saclay. We are honoured to welcome him to share a selection of recent developments and perspectives in integrable PDEs, offering participants an opportunity to engage closely with his ideas and explore the questions that animate this rapidly developing field.

Held in honour of Professor Gérard, the meeting will also bring together researchers from China and abroad working on integrable systems, fluid dynamics, and dispersive equations. His lectures will provide a focal point for a week of discussion and exchange, connecting the themes of the mini-course with complementary approaches across these fields. Through lectures and informal conversations, we hope to develop a shared understanding of recent advances, identify fruitful connections, and inspire new collaborations.
